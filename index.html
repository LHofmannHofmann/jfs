STEP 4

Keep the conversation going

Listen→Respond→Share→Ask

Tap each one to open it.

1. Listen and respond

Listen to the employer's answer and respond to something they said instead of jumping straight to your next question.

2. Show interest

Make eye contact, smile, and show real interest. Ask a follow-up question when something catches your attention.

3. Share something about you

Look for a connection between what the employer says and your experience, skills, classes, or career goals.

4. Keep it going

Use what you just learned to ask a related follow-up question. Then start the loop again.

You don't need a perfect conversation. Be curious, listen, and look for a connection.
